Game Overview

PAYDAY 2 is a cooperative four-player first-person shooter developed by Overkill Software and released in 2013. Players step into the masks of the PAYDAY Gang as they pull off a wide array of high-stakes criminal contracts across Washington D.C., managed through the dynamic CRIME.NET job network. Heists range from quick jewelry store robberies and bank vault crackings to complex, multi-day operations involving drug manufacturing, smuggling, and high-security infiltrations. Missions can generally be approached in full stealth—using silent weapons, ECM jammers, and body bags—or in loud "loud" combat, where players hold off endless waves of heavily armed police assault teams. Dynamic elements like randomized camera placements, vault locations, keycard spawns, and escape routes give each playthrough high replay value.

Developers

PAYDAY 2 was developed by Swedish studio Overkill Software and published by parent company Starbreeze Studios (initially in partnership with 505 Games). Lead designer David Goldfarb helped shape its early core design, while composer Simon Viklund created its iconic electronic soundtrack. Over its ten-year active post-launch lifecycle, Overkill expanded the game through dozens of DLCs, weapon packs, and community events, maintaining a dedicated player base through continuous content updates.
